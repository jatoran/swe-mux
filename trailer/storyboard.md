## Editorial approach

The edit favors proof over a feature-name wall.
Every major claim sits over footage of the live surface performing or exposing that capability.

Captions arrive from the left with a fast ease-out swoop.
The six-shot utility montage accelerates the middle of the trailer without asking the viewer to read long copy.
The phone is composited at native mobile proportions over a darkened live-UI backdrop.

## Score map

The score is an original 120 BPM industrial synthwave cue generated by `render_feature_cut.py`.
The opening drone establishes tension before the full drums and bass enter with the live workspace.
Half-time breakdowns create room for the utility montage, customization, and the final transition.
Four-second risers and impacts keep the longer cut moving without narration.
The last five seconds resolve into a sustained logo chord and sub impact.
